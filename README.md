# ILAI (இலை) — Sustainable Femcare

**ILAI** is an eco-conscious menstrual care brand dedicated to creating natural, biodegradable sanitary pads crafted from **banana fibre and water hyacinth**.

Rooted in sustainability and care, ILAI offers a gentle, body-friendly alternative to conventional synthetic pads while actively protecting our waterways and planet.

---

## What is ILAI?

Conventional menstrual pads take hundreds of years to decompose and are largely made from non-biodegradable plastics and harsh chemical additives.

**ILAI** reimagines menstrual wellness through circular, plant-based innovation:

- **Plant-Powered Materials**: Thoughtfully crafted using natural plant fibres — banana fibre and water hyacinth.
- **Eco-Friendly & Biodegradable**: Decomposes naturally, reducing single-use plastic waste and environmental footprint.
- **Gentle & Breathable**: Prioritizes skin-friendly comfort, softness, and breathability without harsh chemicals or synthetic irritants.
- **Sustainable Impact**: Upcycles natural biomass and helps clean freshwater ecosystems by utilizing invasive water hyacinth, supporting a cleaner and greener future.

## Our Mission

> *"Gentle on you, kind to the Earth."*

Our mission is to make sustainable, comfortable, and chemical-free menstrual care accessible, proving that everyday wellness and environmental stewardship can go hand in hand.
